Retired browser visualizer

Part III is no longer presented as an interactive browser instrument.

The former Resolution Collapse visualizer has been retired. Part III remains a paper-led terminal work: Detection, Circuit Breakers, Proper Ending, Memory Preserved, and Authority Returned.

StatusBrowser visualizer retired
Primary objectPart III paper and local PDF
Future visual formMoving-image study, not live dashboard

The ending should not become dashboard theatre.

Part III concerns the controlled ending of a governance process when judgment can no longer be verified. A browser visualizer risks making that ending look like a decorative mechanism rather than a terminal argument.

The visual direction should therefore be developed separately as a deliberate moving-image work: quieter, more authored, and less interactive.
