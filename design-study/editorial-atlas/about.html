01 / RESEARCH POSITION

Oversight is a capacity, not a presence.

This work studies the institutional conditions under which consequential human–AI decisions remain governable.

Evidence, time, authority, practical review capacity, fallback and remedy are treated as real conditions of accountable decisions—not interface labels. The trilogy tracks those requirements across individual cases, institutions and accountable exits.

02 / METHODOLOGICAL BOUNDARY

Formalize the claim. Publish the limit.

The research draws on typed protocols, finite-state exploration, synthetic stress exercises and theory-directed analysis of public records. These methods establish bounded claims, not proof of field effectiveness or causal representativeness.
