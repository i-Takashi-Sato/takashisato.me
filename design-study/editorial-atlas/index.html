Where the argument ends.

These are versioned working papers and bounded research artifacts, not a legal determination, compliance certification, operational decision engine or proof of field effectiveness.

Finite checks establish properties only within their declared abstractions. Synthetic results are not measures of real-world sensitivity, specificity or impact. The shared public-record corpus supports traceability, not representative causal inference.
