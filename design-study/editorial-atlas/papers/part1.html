MECHANISM / SCHEMATIC

01 / RESEARCH QUESTION

Can oversight be specified as a case contract?

A person may be formally “in the loop” while lacking admissible evidence, adequate time, authority, review capacity or fallback.

The paper shifts the unit of governance from an AI model alone to the workflow that translates recommendations into consequential institutional decisions. Its central claim is that decision states, constraints and authorizations must be explicitly typed.

02 / THE CONTRACT

Routing is not authorization.

The first stage describes three diagnostic gates: hard constraints and admissibility; value, impact and conflict; and temporal validity and reliability. A gate returns a typed status rather than pretending that missing evidence is an approval or denial.

GATE 01

Admissibility

Scope, prohibitions and minimum evidentiary preconditions.

GATE 02

Impact and conflict

Proportionality, material conflicts and rights-sensitive trade-offs.

GATE 03

Temporal validity

Evidence, assumptions and policy remain current.

STAGE 02

Signed disposition

Only the duly authorized institution may release, modify, defer, stop or use fallback.

03 / VERIFICATION BOUNDARY

Finite checks, bounded conclusions.

The formal checks examine the grammar within a declared abstraction. They are not demonstrations of performance in deployed institutions.

04 / CLAIM LIMIT

Consistency is not legal sufficiency.

Verification of the specified state machine cannot prove that a real institution has adequate staff, resources, lawful authority, reliable evidence or effective remedy. The research record states these limitations.
