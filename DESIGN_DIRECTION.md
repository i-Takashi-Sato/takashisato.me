# The Proper Ending Index — Editorial Atlas v8

The website remains an independent scholarly research archive. Published manuscripts, identifiers, preservation hashes, canonical routes, and the paper version v6.2 are not a design surface.

## The research is the interface
The three independently meaningful mechanisms—typed case routing, governing-capacity loss under procedural continuity, and accountable retirement with authority return—organize typography, pacing, figures and navigation. They are not one degradation index or a simulated workflow.

## Visual system
- Ivory editorial canvas and precise dark-ink typography for the archive and first two papers.
- Deep ink for the third paper, treating closure as an obligation rather than spectacle.
- Three distinct diagrams on Home; each is labelled schematic and never represents observed measurements.
- Newsreader and Inter via existing self-hosted fonts; no external assets.
- Plain bibliographic entry points to SSRN, preserved PDFs, DOI and stated claim boundaries.
- No fake signatures, invented endorsements, dashboard statistics or ornamental gauges.

## Engineering contract
Static HTML, CSS and progressive enhancement. The source model owns paper metadata. Research pages, canonical, JSON-LD and PDF records must be preserved verbatim in v8. All navigation works without JavaScript. Keyboard, narrow viewport, contrast, reduced-motion and print behavior take priority over animation.

## Release discipline
Review screenshots and CI before merging any redesign branch. Do not change main or deploy without explicit approval. Aesthetic claims must not be confused with tests of research validity.
