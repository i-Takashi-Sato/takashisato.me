Why this archive exists

A site for systems that look governed after judgment has left.

This archive studies AI governance at the point where policy, workflow, human attention, and institutional pressure meet.

FieldAI Governance
LensWorkflow, bureaucracy, failure legibility
AuthorTakashi Sato / Independent Researcher

Not an AI lab. Not a product page. A research index.

The work focuses less on model behavior than on the surrounding institution: the gates, records, exceptions, latencies, incentives, silences, and stopping conditions through which human judgment is either preserved or converted into procedure.

The archive is intentionally positioned between research writing, institutional design, and visual explanation. Its purpose is not to simulate authority, but to make silent governance failure easier to read.

The central concern is simple: an AI-assisted process can keep running while the human capacity to contest it has already collapsed.

Position

Model-centric governance is insufficient.

The last mile of AI governance is a workflow problem, not only a model problem.
