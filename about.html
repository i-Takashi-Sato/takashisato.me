About the archive

Who keeps the loop thinking?

Takashi Sato is an independent researcher working at the intersection of AI governance, workflow design, human oversight, and operational risk control.

Position

This archive studies how AI-assisted organizations fail not only through misuse, but through ordinary workflows: fatigue, ritualized oversight, semantic decay, dissent exhaustion, and the loss of meaningful human judgment.

The work combines independent research papers, conceptual visualizations, and an operational lens drawn from business operations, workflow standardization, SOP design, and risk control.

Research sequence
Part I Structured friction

ALTRION reframes human oversight as a workflow architecture: gates, warnings, arbitration, and auditable decision records.

Part II Pre-abuse failure

Governance can collapse before bad actors arrive, through cognitive economy, responsibility inversion, entropy, and legibility capture.

Part III Failure legibility

Resolution collapse, Governance Drift Indicators, and circuit breakers define when a system must stop to preserve institutional memory.

Practical lens

The archive is designed for readers concerned with AI deployment, auditability, operational risk, process design, and the fragile boundary between human judgment and procedural theater.
