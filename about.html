About the archive

Why this archive exists.

This site studies a specific institutional failure: systems that remain procedurally intact while the human capacity to distinguish, contest, and stop decisions quietly declines.

AuthorTakashi Sato
FieldAI governance
FrameWorkflow design
QuestionWhen must governance stop?

Not a lab site. Not a dashboard.

The Proper Ending Index is a research archive for workflow-centric AI governance. Its concern is not merely whether a model is accurate, but whether the institution around the model can still think.

The papers treat oversight as an operational surface: gates, records, warnings, delays, exceptions, review language, dissent, and the eventual right to stop.

The visual system is deliberately editorial rather than cybernetic. It is designed to read like a research index, not like a monitoring product.

Position

The archive is a reading instrument.

Operating claim.

AI governance should be judged not only by uninterrupted operation, but by whether the system can recognize the loss of meaningful judgment and preserve institutional memory when it stops.
