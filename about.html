About the archive

A research room for systems that fail quietly.

The Archive Instrument studies the operational failure modes of human oversight in AI-assisted institutions. Its focus is not only model behavior, but the surrounding workflow: the gates, rituals, exceptions, latency, silence, and decision records through which organizations either preserve judgment or lose it.

The work is intentionally positioned between research archive, institutional design, and visual system. The site treats governance as something that must be read, tested, visualized, and eventually stopped when it no longer thinks.
