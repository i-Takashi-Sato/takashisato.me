Part II examines how governance architecture decays when organizations optimize for cognitive economy, liability coverage, and administrative legibility.

Problem field

The dangerous failure is not always abuse. Sometimes it is ordinary operation.

Pre-abuse failure names the moment when a system becomes brittle before any malicious use appears.

Organizations do not need bad actors to erode oversight. They need time pressure, ambiguous responsibility, fatigue, incentives for smooth reporting, and a process that rewards administrative completion.

Part II treats governance decay as a thermodynamic problem: the system tends toward lower-effort, lower-friction states unless counter-pressure is deliberately maintained.

The result is not dramatic collapse. It is the gradual normalization of cognitive offload, liability hardening, and legibility capture.

RiskProcedure absorbs judgment while preserving the appearance of responsible review.
MechanismCognitive economy converts complex evaluation into administratively convenient resolution.
WarningThe workflow can become safer to follow than to question.

Failure trajectory

The pre-abuse path is a sequence of cheaper substitutions.

Each phase replaces difficult judgment with a form that is easier to process, defend, or measure.

Phase 01

Cognitive offload.

Human judgment is transferred to the system because the system is easier to trust than to re-evaluate.

Effort reduction
Phase 02

Liability hardening.

The process becomes safer to defend than to understand.

Responsibility inversion
Phase 03

Operational entropy.

Edge cases, fatigue, and exception handling are absorbed into routine administrative flow.

Friction decay
Phase 04

Legibility capture.

The record becomes more important than the judgment it was supposed to preserve.

Measurement capture

Evidence plate

Decay becomes visible when the record starts defending the process.

The warning sign is not absence of documentation. It is documentation that can no longer prove meaningful contestability.

PRE-ABUSE-TRACE-002

Procedure complete. Judgment uncertain.

The institution can say the process was followed. It can no longer show that the process remained capable of serious interruption.
