Author abstract · v6.3

Abstract

An AI-assisted institution may fulfill its procedural obligations while independent epistemic contribution, practical corrective authority, and the performance of a protected function deteriorate. Procedural completion alone then provides little assurance that human control remains intact. This paper develops a middle-range model of this pattern. Governing-capacity loss under procedural continuity (GCL-PC) is a non-compensatory profile of four jointly established conditions within a declared workflow, population and interval: stable or improving procedural fidelity; material and persistent decline in independent judgment; material and persistent decline in institutional actionability; and material impairment of a predeclared protected function. Each condition is coded established, not met or indeterminate, so that missing evidence is not mistaken for evidence of sound governance. The profile implies neither concealment nor a particular cause. A separate causal question asks whether, under a well-defined intervention that disables a named adversarial pathway, a declared ordinary organizational exposure materially harms the protected outcome; identification status and effect assessment are reported on separate axes. Four candidate mechanisms, five testable propositions with rejection conditions, a measurement protocol that separates contribution from actionability, and retention and retirement rules that must be preregistered before evaluation define a research program. Fifteen public-record units from three official investigations of automated decision systems predating generative AI show only that objects associated with the proposed mechanisms are traceable; no field validation, individual causation, prevalence or predictive superiority is claimed. Whether the profile adds value beyond means–ends decoupling and effective-oversight frameworks is an empirical hypothesis, and the binary label must be retired for any use in which it fails to improve a declared decision beyond its continuous components and established baselines.

Research question

Can procedure remain visible after governing capacity has declined?

Part II models a joint institutional state in which observable process remains stable or improves while independent judgment, practical actionability, and a predeclared protected function materially deteriorate.

The primary construct is GCL-PC: governing-capacity loss under procedural continuity. It implies neither concealment nor a particular cause. The ordinary-exposure causal question is separate from the profile.

Descriptive profile model

Four objects must remain separate.

Declared workflow · protected function · population · interval I

formative, non-interchangeable elements
PProcedural fidelityVisible steps, approvals, documentation, and service-level completion.
JJudgment contributionCase-level evidence or reasoning beyond the AI output and template.
GGoverning capacityPractical ability to alter, resource, escalate, remedy, or stop.
NProtected functionAn independently measured, predeclared outcome—not model accuracy by default.
GCL-PC established iff FP, DJ, DG, IN are all established.

Each condition is established, not met, or indeterminate. Of 81 profiles, one establishes GCL-PC, 65 do not meet it, and 15 remain indeterminate. Not met does not mean healthy. A separate causal specification assesses an ordinary exposure under an intervention disabling a named adversarial pathway, reporting identification and effect separately.

Four candidate mechanisms

Pathways to loss, not a closed taxonomy.

01

Cognitive externalization

Reviewers increasingly treat the model output as both the starting and ending representation of the case, displacing complementary scrutiny.

02

Legibility capture

Actors optimize countable completion, agreement, or documentation artifacts while the protected function deteriorates.

03

Responsibility inversion

Formal accountability concentrates on the reviewer who bears the cost of deviation but lacks power over the system.

04

Voice attenuation

Unremedied or punished concerns reduce the future supply of dissent and deprive the institution of corrective information.

The set is provisional and theory-directed. Deskilling, vendor lock-in, update opacity, dependency, resource withdrawal, or other mechanisms should be added or preferred when they improve identification.

Falsifiability

Five derived propositions—and what would count against them.

Claim boundary

Classification is not a detector or an accusation.

  • The framework does not establish that any real institution satisfies GCL-PC.
  • A causal contrast requires a defined intervention and identification assumptions, not retrospective selection on the absence of documented adversarial conduct.
  • The shared fifteen-unit official-record corpus supports theory-directed traceability, not frequency, necessity, sufficiency, prediction, or independent triangulation.
  • Thresholds, measurement validity, site transportability, causal identification, and intervention utility remain empirical questions.
