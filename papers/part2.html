Structure: Framing → Pre-abuse Failure → Four Failure Trajectories → Design Targets

Abstract

Current AI governance discourse focuses on adversarial robustness, regulatory non-compliance, and malicious misuse. This paper argues that such framing overlooks a more pervasive risk: pre-abuse failure.

Pre-abuse failure is the structural collapse of governance mechanisms not through malice or attack, but through organizational entropy and cognitive economy.

Using ALTRION as an analytical probe, the paper identifies four self-reinforcing trajectories: Cognitive Externalization, Responsibility Inversion, Organizational Entropy, and Legibility Capture.

Why it matters

  • Reframes risk from misuse to collapse-before-misuse.
  • Names the mechanism: entropy plus cognitive economy, not adversarial cleverness.
  • Provides a design target for governance architectures that survive good-faith operation at scale.
