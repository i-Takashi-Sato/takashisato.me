Forecasting Failure

The system shatters under its own weight before it is ever abused.悪用される前に、自らの重みで砕け散る。

Published2025/12/13
SSRN5913703

PhaseDecay

The danger is not only malicious misuse. It is competent routine.

Current AI governance discourse focuses on adversarial robustness, regulatory non-compliance, and malicious misuse. This paper argues that such framing overlooks a more pervasive risk: pre-abuse failure.

Pre-abuse failure is the structural collapse of governance mechanisms not through malice or attack, but through organizational entropy and cognitive economy.

  • Risk shifts from misuse to collapse-before-misuse.
  • The mechanism is entropy plus cognitive economy.
  • Good-faith operation can still destroy oversight.
Trajectory 01

Cognitive Externalization.

Judgment migrates out of the human loop while the ritual of review remains.

Trajectory 02

Responsibility Inversion.

Humans become accountable for outputs they no longer meaningfully determine.

Trajectory 03

Organizational Entropy.

Exceptions, records, and review practices decay under operational pressure.

Trajectory 04

Legibility Capture.

What is machine-readable replaces what is situationally true.

Part II turns ALTRION from solution into diagnostic probe.

Analytical move

A well-designed system is placed under ordinary institutional pressure to reveal how governance becomes ritualized.

Design target

Governance must be engineered to survive cognitive economy, not only hostile misuse.

Extract / Part IIPre-abuse failure

Organizational entropy does not announce itself. It converts review into routine, dissent into silence, and exceptions into speed bumps.
