Part III / Termination

Detecting Silent
Governance Failure

Published2026/01/13
SSRN6066430
Revision2026/01/13
“Silence is the scream of a failing system.”沈黙とは、破綻へ向かう装置の咆哮。

Structure: Resolution Collapse → Governance Drift Indicators → Circuit Breakers → Proper Ending

Abstract

Current AI governance often focuses on the noise of failure: errors, bias, and misuse. In high-stakes bureaucracy, the most catastrophic failures often manifest as silence.

Resolution Collapse describes a condition where human oversight persists as procedural ritual while the system loses the friction required to distinguish routine cases from exceptions.

The paper proposes Governance Drift Indicators and Circuit Breakers to detect and halt AI-assisted processes when epistemic capacity is lost, preserving institutional memory through a proper ending.

Why it matters

  • Makes silence measurable through Governance Drift Indicators.
  • Shifts the focus from fixing errors to maintaining legibility.
  • Defines the endgame: circuit breakers and controlled termination.
