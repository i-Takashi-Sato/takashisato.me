Part III / Termination

Detecting Silent Governance Failure

Silence is the scream of a failing system.沈黙とは、破綻へ向かう装置の咆哮。

Published2026/01/13
SSRN6066430
Revision2026/01/13
PhaseTermination

The worst failure is quiet enough to look like governance.

Current AI governance often focuses on the noise of failure: errors, bias, and misuse. In high-stakes bureaucracy, the most catastrophic failures often manifest as silence.

Resolution Collapse describes a condition where human oversight persists as procedural ritual while the system loses the friction required to distinguish routine cases from exceptions.

  • Silence becomes measurable through Governance Drift Indicators.
  • Legibility matters more than smooth continuation.
  • Circuit Breakers preserve memory through controlled termination.
Indicator 01

Semantic Entropy Decay.

Human explanations lose information and collapse into template language.

Indicator 02

Temporal Compression.

Complex and routine cases begin to move through the process at the same speed.

Indicator 03

Exhaustion of Dissent.

Overrides, modifications, and escalations disappear without proof of improvement.

Endpoint

Proper Ending.

When judgment can no longer be verified, the responsible action is controlled suspension.

Failure legibility is the last defense before ritual becomes authority.

Resolution Collapse

Oversight remains visible as a procedure while its contestability has already disappeared.

Circuit Breaker

The process must be stopped when its outputs can no longer be connected to meaningful human judgment.

Return to the index.

The sequence ends by reframing governance as the capacity to detect, explain, and stop a system that still appears to function.
