Part III turns the archive into a stopping instrument: not a warning display, but a decision surface for ending failed oversight correctly.

Problem field

The worst failure is quiet enough to look like governance.

Noise is easy to detect. Silence is harder, because silence can look like stability, consensus, or successful compliance.

Part III treats silent governance failure as the point where oversight remains visible as a procedure while its contestability has already disappeared.

Resolution Collapse occurs when a system can still generate documents, approvals, logs, and explanations, but those artifacts no longer prove that human judgment is operating.

The response is not more decoration, more dashboards, or more confidence language. The response is a controlled ability to detect drift, preserve memory, and stop.

RiskFailure becomes ordinary because nothing appears to demand interruption.
MeasureTrack silence through semantic decay, temporal compression, and exhaustion of dissent.
BoundaryIf judgment cannot be verified, continuation is not governance. It is ritual.

Governance Drift Indicators

Silent failure becomes visible only when the archive measures what procedure hides.

The indicators do not predict evil. They detect the loss of contestability inside apparently normal operation.

GDI-01

Semantic Entropy Decay.

Explanations lose information and collapse into template language.

Meaning loss
GDI-02

Temporal Compression.

Complex and routine cases begin to move through the process at the same speed.

Time distortion
GDI-03

Exhaustion of Dissent.

Overrides, escalations, and modifications disappear without evidence that quality improved.

Contestability loss
CB-01

Circuit Breaker.

The process is suspended when outputs can no longer be tied to meaningful human judgment.

Controlled termination

Evidence plate

The proper ending preserves memory before authority returns.

Stopping is not failure. Stopping is the governance act that prevents failed procedure from continuing to speak with institutional authority.

Proper Ending Record

Detection plus memory plus controlled suspension.

The system is not merely halted. Its failure conditions are preserved so the organization cannot reactivate the same ritual under a new name.
