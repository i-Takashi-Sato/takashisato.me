Part I / Architecture

Workflow-Centric AI Governance

To preserve the human, we must engineer the friction of thought.思考に摩擦を仕込むという設計。

Published2025/12/12
SSRN5911063
Revision2026/01/09
PhaseArchitecture

The problem is not only model output. It is the workflow that receives it.

AI integration into high-stakes domains has revealed a sociotechnical gap: model performance can improve while institutional capacity to operationalize AI outputs remains fragile.

Without procedural safeguards, human oversight can become performative ritual rather than substantive review. ALTRION responds by treating governance as an auditable decision pipeline.

  • Oversight becomes a workflow object.
  • Structured friction preserves cognitive engagement.
  • Governance shifts from policy decoration to operational architecture.
Gate 01

Baseline constraints.

Define the minimum governance conditions before optimization begins.

Gate 02

Optimization balance.

Prevent efficiency from silently displacing human contestability.

Gate 03

Temporal context.

Force the system to treat time, drift, and case history as governance facts.

Gate 04

Human arbitration.

Keep the final interpretive act visible, contestable, and recorded.

ALTRION is not the ending. It is the baseline from which decay can be seen.

Design target

Introduce enough structured friction to make human judgment visible without converting oversight into bureaucratic theatre.

Failure threshold

The paper identifies the point at which oversight collapses into procedure and prepares the ground for Part II.
