Part I defines the minimum architecture required before an AI-enabled decision process can claim accountable oversight.

Problem field

The model is not the whole governance problem.

The workflow that receives the model output determines whether oversight remains a human act or becomes a ritual of documentation.

AI integration into high-stakes domains can improve formal performance while weakening the institution’s ability to notice when judgment has already been displaced.

Part I treats governance as an auditable decision pipeline. The point is not to decorate AI adoption with policy language, but to design friction, delay, contestability, and final human authority into the operational route.

ALTRION is introduced here as an architectural baseline: a way to make oversight inspectable before the later papers examine how that baseline decays.

RiskOptimization can quietly replace deliberation while the organization still appears compliant.
ResponseConvert oversight into a workflow object with checkpoints, warnings, rationale, and a final auditable decision record.
BoundaryThe architecture is not a cure. It is the measuring surface from which later failure becomes visible.

Four-Gate Architecture

Oversight survives only when the route is designed.

The four gates are not decorative checkpoints. Each gate names the condition that must remain inspectable before, during, and after the decision process.

Gate 01

Baseline constraints.

Define the minimum governance conditions before optimization begins.

Entry condition
Gate 02

Optimization balance.

Prevent efficiency metrics from silently displacing human contestability.

Balance check
Gate 03

Temporal context.

Force the workflow to treat time, drift, and case history as governance facts.

Freshness check
Gate 04

Human arbitration.

Keep the final interpretive act visible, contestable, and recorded.

Authority return

Evidence plate

The trace matters after the workflow moves on.

The Auditable Decision Record preserves warnings, rationale, and the final decision as an inspectable object rather than a decorative log.

ALTRION-ADR-001

Warnings plus rationale plus final authority.

The record is useful only if it survives the moment of decision. It must remain readable after the organization has moved on.
