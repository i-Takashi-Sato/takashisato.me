This archive studies where human judgment fails inside AI-assisted workflows: not only through misuse, but through compliance decay, semantic entropy, dissent exhaustion, and procedural silence.

Applied Frame

For organizations adopting AI, the central question is not whether humans remain in the loop. It is whether the loop still thinks.

The work is positioned for AI governance, operational risk, workflow design, auditability, and institutional failure detection in AI-assisted decision systems.
