THE PROPER ENDING INDEX / 01–03

A role alone
is not governance.

Accountability lives in the sequence.

Three working papers examine how consequential human–AI decisions are routed, how governing capacity can deteriorate while procedures remain intact, and how failing workflows can be retired without abandoning unresolved obligations. They propose analytical models and bounded verification, not demonstrated operational effectiveness.

The research sequence

From a case decision to an institutional exit.

The papers are connected, but they do not collapse into a single score or universal lifecycle. Each asks a distinct question and states its own claim boundary.

Part I · Route the case

Workflow-Centric AI Governance

What may happen next? A falsifiable, two-stage state-and-authority grammar for routing consequential human-AI decisions without turning missing evidence, unavailable capacity, or preliminary machine states into silent approval or denial.

Typed case contract · v6.2 · 18 pages

Part II · Diagnose the institution

Procedural Continuity and Governing-Capacity Loss in AI-Assisted Institutions

Can it still govern? A descriptive state model for institutions whose visible procedure remains intact while independent judgment, practical governing capacity, and a predeclared protected function materially deteriorate.

Descriptive state model · v6.2 · 20 pages

Part III · End and return authority

From Governance Drift to Accountable Exit

How does it end accountably? A dependency-constrained protocol for containing and retiring AI-assisted workflows while preserving service, evidence, remedy, accountable ownership, and the practical capacity to decide.

Exit and authority protocol · v6.2 · 39 pages

Part I / Finite contract

v6.2 independent reimplementation: 1,024 Stage-1 and 1,248 applicable Stage-2 configurations; nine isolated guard mutations. Truth-table abstraction and invariant checks.

Part II / Descriptive model

Five rejectable propositions and a comparative retirement test. Empirical rejection criteria, not a validated classification.

Part III / Transition protocol

Frozen v5.9 execution, carried forward in v6.2: 8,564 reachable states, 36,096 enabled transitions and 30 guard mutations within a selected finite abstraction. Synthetic benchmark results are also carried forward; no new execution is claimed in v6.2. Verification scope and disclosed execution provenance.

These quantities describe different research objects. They are not comparable measures of validity, performance or practical effectiveness.

What this archive does not claim.

These are working papers and formalized research artifacts—not legal advice, a compliance certification, an operational decision engine, or evidence that the proposed architectures improve outcomes in the field.

Finite checks are exhaustive only over each declared abstraction. The shared public-record corpus supports traceability, not frequency, prediction, causal sufficiency, or independent validation.

Independent critical review is invited. Examine the review questions and submission route.
