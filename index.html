Verification register.

Part I / Finite contract

Part I reports 1,024 Stage-1 and 1,144 Stage-2 inputs and an integrated model with 134,672 reachable states. It is not an independent third-party replication. Truth-table abstraction and invariant checks.

Part II / Descriptive model

Four three-valued conditions yield 81 descriptive profiles; five rejectable propositions and a comparative retirement test. Criteria for empirical rejection, not evidence of a validated classification.

Part III / Transition protocol

The historical model has 8,564 states and 36,096 transitions; 1,152 states have no path to closure, including 288 stranded retirement-authorized states. Revised bounded PE-R1 has 98,948 reachable states. Verification scope and disclosed execution provenance.

These quantities describe different research objects. They are not comparable measures of validity, performance or practical effectiveness.

Limits of the featured research.

These are working papers and formalized research artifacts—not legal advice, a compliance certification, an operational decision engine, or evidence that the proposed architectures improve outcomes in the field.

Finite checks are exhaustive only over each declared abstraction. The shared public-record corpus supports traceability, not frequency, prediction, causal sufficiency, or independent validation.
