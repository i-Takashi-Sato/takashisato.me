Central Thesis

Governance is not a policy layer. It is an operating architecture.

Human oversight collapses when procedures remain visible but judgment disappears. The archive studies that failure as a design problem: how to preserve friction, dissent, exception handling, and institutional memory inside AI-assisted workflows.
